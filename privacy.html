Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Shorty Panel ("the app") is a desktop application for managing the production and the analytics of short-form videos. This policy explains what data the app accesses when you connect an account, how that data is handled, and how you can remove it.

1. Data the app accesses

When you connect your TikTok account, the app uses TikTok's official APIs, with only the permissions (scopes) that you grant on TikTok's authorization page:

PermissionDataPurpose in the app
user.info.basicOpen ID, display name, avatarIdentify which TikTok account is connected to the panel.
user.info.profileBio description, profile link, verification statusDisplay the profile of the connected channel in the panel.
user.info.statsFollower count, likes count, following count, video countShow the account's aggregate KPIs in the dashboard.
video.listYour public videos and their basic metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, duration, cover, link, creation date)Populate the content table and build the account's own statistics history.
video.uploadUpload of video files you selectSend a finished video to your own TikTok inbox as a draft, so you can review and post it from the TikTok app.

If you connect other platforms (for example YouTube, Instagram or Facebook) to the panel, the app accesses only the equivalent statistics of those accounts, through each platform's official APIs and with the permissions you grant there.

2. How the data is stored

  • Access tokens, refresh tokens and the data retrieved from the APIs are stored locally on your own device (configuration files and a local database).
  • The data is not uploaded to third-party servers, not used for advertising, not sold and not shared with anyone.
  • Video files are only transferred to TikTok when you explicitly confirm the upload from the panel.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Access tokens remain stored until you revoke them or delete them.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Security and permissions). Once revoked, the app can no longer read your data.
  • You can delete all locally stored data by removing the app's configuration file and database from your device, or by writing to the contact address below.

4. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Espanola de Proteccion de Datos).

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended to be used by anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or in the applicable regulations. The current version is always published on this page, with its date.
