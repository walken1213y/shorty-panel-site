Shorty Panel

A desktop workspace for short-form video: plan each episode, follow its production, review the analytics of your connected accounts, and send the finished video to TikTok as a draft for final review.

What it does

Shorty Panel is a local desktop application that coordinates the production of short vertical videos and the performance of the accounts where they are published. Everything runs on your own machine, and every platform connection is authorized by you through that platform's official sign-in page.

Production tracking

Each episode is followed through every phase of its production (script, voice-over, render, assembly) with the status of each platform where it has been published.

Analytics dashboard

Connect your own accounts and see views, interactions and follower counts of your content in one place. The panel only reads the data of the accounts you explicitly authorize.

Draft publishing to TikTok

When an episode is finished, send it from the panel to your own TikTok account as a draft. The video arrives in your TikTok inbox, where you review it, edit it and decide whether to post it. Shorty Panel never publishes on your behalf.

Local and private

Credentials and access tokens are stored only on your own device. Analytics data is not uploaded to third-party servers, sold or shared.

How it works

  1. Connect your account. Sign in with your TikTok account from the panel through TikTok's official authorization page. You choose which permissions to grant and you can revoke them at any time from your TikTok settings.
  2. Produce the episode. The panel keeps track of every phase of the video production and of the content already published on each platform.
  3. Send the video to TikTok drafts. Select the finished video in the panel and confirm the upload. The video is delivered to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. Review and post. Open the TikTok app, review the draft, add the description and the sound, and publish it yourself.

FAQ

Does Shorty Panel publish to TikTok automatically?
No. The panel only sends your finished video to your own TikTok account as a draft. Publishing always happens from the TikTok app, after you review the video.
What TikTok data does the panel access?
Only your own account's data: basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar), profile details (bio and profile link), aggregate statistics (followers, likes, following and video count) and the list of your public videos with their metrics. This information is displayed in your local panel and is not shared with anyone.
Can I revoke access?
Yes, at any time from your TikTok account settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
